## Projects

### [Weather Market Forecasting Engine](https://github.com/asnobani/weather-market-forecaster)
`Python` · `SQLite` · `statistical modeling` · zero dependencies

Blends four global weather ensembles into calibrated probability forecasts and evaluates them against live prediction-market prices. Ran 21 days across 7 US cities over 508 simulated trades.

**The result was negative, and that's the point:** the model out-forecast the market (Brier 0.106 vs 0.117) and still lost money after fees. Transaction costs and adverse selection explain the gap. The pass/fail criteria were fixed before the trial began — the strategy was not funded.
